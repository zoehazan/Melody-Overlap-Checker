How to read the results

Close match

At least 8 consecutive intervals line up almost exactly, usually with the same rhythm. Listen to both songs side by side.

Worth a listen

A shorter or looser stretch lines up. Often harmless, but compare by ear before release.

Passing resemblance

Short or mostly stepwise overlap. Scales, repeated notes and simple arpeggios appear in thousands of songs.

Leaps count for more than steps, because a distinctive leap is what makes a melody recognizable. For a release with real stakes, a forensic musicologist is the right next step.
